Happy Birthday, Urbbi 🎂

Pata hai, main ye sab aamne-saamne bolne mein thoda sa hero nahi hoon. Bolne jaata hoon toh atak jaata hoon. Isliye socha, likh ke deta hoon — likha hua kabhi atakta nahi.

Ek time tha… main sach mein bahut akela tha. Matlab aisa akela, ki phone haath mein hota tha par call karne ko koi naam nahi hota tha. Baat karne wala koi dost nahi tha. Sab kuch andar hi andar chal raha tha, aur main bas din kaat raha tha. Tab tu aayi.

Aur pata nahi kab, dheere-dheere, tu meri woh person ban gayi jisse main sabse pehle baat karna chahta hoon. Kuch bhi ho — achha, bura, ya bilkul hi bakwaas — sabse pehle tera hi khayal aata hai.

Hum kitne door hain, ye toh pata hi hai. Screen ke uss paar se hi saari dosti chali hai. Phir bhi ajeeb baat ye hai ki tu sabse paas hai. Distance ne kabhi kuch bigaada hi nahi — shayad isiliye, kyunki dosti dil se hoti hai, kilometres se nahi. (Ye line thodi filmy ho gayi, par sach hai toh hai.)

Aaj tu 18 ki ho gayi. Officially badi. License, vote, sab kuch. Par mere liye tu wahi rahegi — meri rasmalai, meri maalkin jii, meri cutie pie, sweetheart… aur mera bacha. Naam bade ho gaye hain, tu mat badalna. Waisi hi rehna — hasne wali, gussa karne wali, meri bakwaas jhelne wali.

Cake maine yahi kaat diya hai virtually. Asli wala due raha — jab kabhi milenge, tab pakka khilaaunga. Aur haan, thank you. Joke wala nahi, sach wala thank you — us time mein aane ke liye jab mere paas koi nahi tha.

Happy birthday ek baar phir. Tera saal itna achha ho, jitni achhi tu hai. (Zyada bhi ho jaaye toh chalega.)
